About Us

SamDAT is the abbreviated form of Sambhavnaa Disability Action Trust. In day-to-day communication, we are known as Sambhavnaa Trust.

The Trust works for the inclusion, wellbeing, and empowerment of persons with disabilities, including people with visual impairment, low vision, and multiple disabilities.

Our work focuses on awareness, accessibility, education, training, employment readiness, and equal participation in society. We believe that opportunity should be shaped by ability and aspiration, not by barriers.

Sambhavnaa Trust is committed to working with individuals, communities, institutions, and like-minded organizations to promote dignity, independence, and barrier-free access across India.
