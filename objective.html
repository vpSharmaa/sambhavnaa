Our Objectives

Sambhavnaa Trust works for the wellbeing, prosperity, dignity, and equal participation of persons with disabilities across India, with a special focus on people with visual impairment and low vision.

Core Objectives

  1. Provide a platform for persons with visual impairment, low vision, and other disabilities recognized under the Rights of Persons with Disabilities Act, 2016.
  2. Promote equal access to opportunities and services for persons with disabilities as full participants in society and the economy.
  3. Raise awareness and understanding of disability issues through training, job readiness, and equal opportunity initiatives.
  4. Strive for equality of opportunity in education, training, and employment.
  5. Identify, explore, and promote new avenues of employment for persons with disabilities.
  6. Promote the production and distribution of educational, recreational, and professional material in accessible media.
  7. Undertake public education to share information about the potential of persons with disabilities as students, workers, and citizens.
  8. Establish, sponsor, or collaborate with agencies to set up institutions and services that support the Trust's objectives.
  9. Cooperate with local, national, and international agencies or like-minded individuals working toward similar goals.
  10. Assist and guide branches of the Trust so they can work effectively toward the Trust's aims.
  11. Work toward barrier-free and accessible public facilities.
  12. Promote education for persons with disabilities in line with modern and practical concepts.
  13. Undertake or promote research that supports progress in rehabilitation and inclusion.
  14. Establish branches in any part of India as decided by the Trust.
  15. Raise funds through subscriptions, donations, grants, bequests, or other lawful methods.

Corporate Social Responsibility (CSR) Objectives

  1. Support efforts related to hunger, poverty, malnutrition, preventive health care, sanitation, and safe drinking water.
  2. Promote education, special education, vocational skills, and livelihood enhancement, especially for children, women, elderly people, and persons with disabilities.
  3. Promote gender equality, empower women, support homes and hostels, senior citizen facilities, and measures for socially or economically disadvantaged groups.
  4. Protect national heritage, art, culture, public libraries, traditional art, and handicrafts.
  5. Support measures for armed forces veterans, war widows, and their dependents, including eligible CAPF and CPMF veterans and dependents.
  6. Promote rural sports, nationally recognized sports, paralympic sports, and olympic sports.
  7. Contribute to lawful relief and welfare funds for socio-economic development and support of disadvantaged communities.
  8. Support incubators, research, and development projects in science, technology, engineering, and medicine where permitted by applicable law.
  9. Support rural development projects and slum area development.
  10. Undertake other activities of general public utility permitted under applicable CSR and income tax provisions.
  11. Do all lawful acts that are incidental or conducive to the attainment of the Trust's purposes.

The Trust's area of operation is the whole of India, with priority given to areas near its offices and branches.
