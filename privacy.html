Privacy

Sambhavnaa Disability Action Trust respects the privacy of visitors and people who contact us through this website.

Information We Receive

We may receive personal information when a visitor contacts us by email, phone, or another communication channel. This may include a name, phone number, email address, and the details shared in the message.

How We Use Information

Information shared with the Trust is used to respond to queries, consider suggestions, coordinate relevant support, or communicate about the Trust's work.

Confidentiality

We aim to handle shared information responsibly. Personal details are not published or shared publicly unless required by law or clearly permitted by the person concerned.
